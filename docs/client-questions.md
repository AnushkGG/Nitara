# Nitaara Tower — Client Meeting & Clarification Questions

**Prepared for:** Builder Pitch Meeting (ABC Corp / Mittal Group / Success Group)  
**Objective:** Confirm exact architectural and sales policy parameters to lock Phase 2–6 production details.

---

### Question 1: Exact Sanctioned Tower Height
* **Current Evidence:** The project is branded as *"The Tallest Landmark of Nigdi Pradhikaran"*, with G + 25 residential floors + Level 26 Sky Retreat (~76m–82m total vertical height).
* **Question for Client:** What is the sanctioned architectural height in metres to display in the technical specifications bar (e.g. "82.4 m / 270 ft")? If you prefer not to disclose numerical metres, we will feature the tagline *"The Tallest Landmark of Nigdi Pradhikaran"*.

### Question 2: Unit Allocation Across Floors & Phase 1 Inventory
* **Current Evidence:** The First Floor Plan confirms 3 units per floor (Flat X01: 3 BHK Type 01, Flat X02: 3 BHK Type 02, Flat X03: 4 BHK). MahaRERA currently shows 36 registered units (Floors 1 to 12).
* **Question for Client:** Are all 25 residential floors identical in floor layout (3 units/floor), or are upper floors (e.g. floors 20–25) customized into penthouses or duplexes? Also, should the public prototype show all 25 floors as interactive, or limit selection to Phase 1 units?

### Question 3: Floor Numbering & Omitted Numbers
* **Current Evidence:** Many Pune luxury towers omit Floor 13 (or 14).
* **Question for Client:** Does Nitaara Tower follow sequential floor numbering (1 to 25), or is Floor 13 omitted / labeled differently (e.g., 12A)?

### Question 4: Pricing Display & Availability Policy
* **Current Evidence:** Current web marketing specifies flat all-inclusive pricing: 3 BHK ₹1.85 Cr, 4 BHK ₹2.36 Cr.
* **Question for Client:** Should the interactive floor explorer display real-time availability states (e.g. Available / Reserved / Sold) driven by your CRM, or should every unit feature a direct "Enquire Now / Schedule Site Visit" action with automatic pre-filled unit details?

### Question 5: Primary Sales Contact & Routing
